Tab & Tick

Privacy

Tab & Tick is a to-do list that works with Google Tasks, as a website and as a Chrome extension. It has no server of its own, and its developer doesn’t receive or store your tasks.

What it uses

When you log in, you allow Tab & Tick to see and change your Google Tasks: your lists and their tasks, including titles, notes, due dates and whether they’re done. It uses these only to show your lists and make the changes you ask for. It doesn’t ask for your name, email, contacts or calendar.

Where it goes

Your tasks go directly between your browser and Google. They aren’t sent to anyone else, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI. No one reads them.

What’s kept in your browser

  • A temporary key from Google. On the website it lasts up to an hour, and is forgotten when you log out or close the tab. In the extension, Chrome looks after it.
  • Which list you last opened, and whether you logged out.

Your tasks aren’t saved in your browser. There are no analytics, adverts or tracking cookies of ours.

Other services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google’s sign-in, so they may record visits as their own policies describe: GitHub and Google.

Stopping access

You can remove Tab & Tick’s access at any time in your Google Account. Your tasks stay in Google Tasks. As we hold no copy, there’s nothing for us to delete.

Google’s rules

Tab & Tick’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Write to tabandtick@googlegroups.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.
