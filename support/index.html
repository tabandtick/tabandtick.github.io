Tab & Tick

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to tabandtick@googlegroups.com, or report it on GitHub. Issues on GitHub are public, so please don’t paste in your own tasks.

Enjoying Tab & Tick?

A review on the Chrome Web Store helps other people find it. It takes a minute, and is much appreciated.

Keyboard shortcuts

In Tab & Tick, press ? when you’re not typing, or Cmd+/ (Ctrl+/ on Windows and Linux). They’re also in the menu under your picture.

On a phone or tablet

Use the website. Hold a task, or choose Select in its menu, to pick it; tap others to add them, then Tick or Delete them from the bar at the bottom. Drag tasks by the dots at their left.

To keep it on your home screen as an app: on an iPhone or iPad, open the website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to home screen or Install app.

Sharing a list

On the website, choose Create shared list in the sidebar, or Share in a list’s menu for a shared copy of it, then add people by their Google account’s email. Google Drive emails them a link that opens the list in Tab & Tick. Google Tasks can’t share lists, so a shared list is kept in a file in your Google Drive instead, and doesn’t appear in Gmail, Calendar or Google’s Tasks app.

Using more than one Google account

On the website, click your picture, then Add another account. Your accounts are listed there to switch between. Log out logs out of the one you’re using.

Adding tasks by voice

On an Android phone, say “Hey Google, add a task to buy milk”, or name a list: “add buy milk to my Shopping list in Google Tasks”. Google adds it to Google Tasks, and it appears in Tab & Tick. Google and Nest speakers work the same way. This works for your Google Tasks lists, not shared lists, and on Google Workspace accounts it needs Google Workspace turned on for Gemini.

On an iPhone, Siri can’t add to Google Tasks yet.

Some of my tasks’ details are missing

Google doesn’t let other apps see stars, repeats, times of day, deadlines, attachments or reminders, so Tab & Tick can’t show or change them. Tasks assigned to you from Google Docs or Chat aren’t shown either. Changing a task in Tab & Tick leaves those details as they were.

A change I made elsewhere isn’t showing

Tab & Tick fetches your lists again when you come back to its tab. If you were in the middle of editing, it waits until next time, so nothing you’re typing is lost. Reloading the page always shows the latest.

It asks me to log in again

In the Chrome extension, Chrome keeps you logged in. On the website, Google’s sign-in lasts an hour, and Tab & Tick renews it on your next click or key near the end of the hour. If the tab is left alone for longer, or you open a new tab, it asks again.

I can’t move a task to another list

A task with subtasks can’t change list, as Google doesn’t allow it. Move its subtasks out first, or move them one at a time.

The extension or the website?

They’re the same app. The Chrome extension opens in every new tab and stays logged in. The website works in any browser, with no install.

Stopping Tab & Tick’s access

Remove the extension from Chrome, and remove Tab & Tick in your Google Account’s connections. Your tasks stay in Google Tasks. See Privacy for what Tab & Tick does with your data.
